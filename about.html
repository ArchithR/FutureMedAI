About Me

Hey, I'm Archith. I'm an 11th grader at Sunset High School, and I built FutureMedAI to explore how software engineering and computer technology intersect with healthcare.

What I Do

Aside from writing code and building web projects, I spend a lot of my time working on robotics. I'm a programmer and CAD mechanical designer for my FIRST Tech Challenge robotics team (High Voltage 18108), where we build and code competitive robots.

Why FutureMedAI

I've always been interested in how technology can solve real-world problems. I created this site as a personal space to write about how artificial intelligence is changing medicine, medical tools, and everyday health tech in a way that's easy to read and understand.
